# Forge of the Elements: Beta 1.4

September 30, 2026.

- New Green Slimes leave poison trails for 10 turns. They start with 12 HP, while Rock Slimes now have 24 base HP and appear on floors 2-4. Both regenerate 2 HP per turn and reproduce when a nonlethal attack hits them at full health, dividing their remaining HP between two full-sized slimes. Either can split again after recovering; only the original awards XP or loot. Poison and Rot stop their regeneration.

- Buried Ghouls lurk unmarked beneath the Crypt floor and can emerge behind you. You get an action to react before their first attack. Green Slimes, Rock Slimes, Ghouls and Myconid Sporecallers have matching native 128-pixel artwork and full animation sets.

- Myconid Sporecallers join the Caverns. Their mushroom buds warn of a 3-by-3 field of roots and poison, then leave green mushrooms that persist until trampled or cleared. Trampling a mushroom deals 2 poison damage. Between casts, Sporecallers retreat, heal an ally for half its maximum HP and grant a temporary Sporecoat shield.

- Support enemies now call a limited number of nearby allies, respect walls and closed doors, and cannot relay calls through a whole floor. Ordinary enemies do not make these calls, and floor 1 is exempt. A creature that hears a call can act on its next scheduled action without spending it waking up.

- Goblin Shamans, Necro-Acolytes, Sporecallers, Drow Priestesses and Lens Bearers position near allies and retreat from close threats. When they cannot attack or make a useful move, they can help an eligible ally instead of idling through a support cooldown. Buffs prefer unbuffed allies; healing an already buffed ally does not refresh its buff.

- Shamans, Sporecallers and Priestesses heal for 50% of an ally's maximum HP, capped at missing health and halved by Rot. Goblin Rage speeds actions, Bone Ward stops the next damaging hit, and Sporecoat provides a temporary shield. Drow Priestesses heal, ward and summon without wasting a turn when no summon tile is available. Lens Bearers keep their ranged support role in Chaos.

- Drow Blades start concealed and reveal when they attack, cast darkness or take damage. Healing and ward effects do not expose them.

- Web Spitters and Chaos Silk Weavers leave 3-by-3 web patches for 10 turns. Walking through silk pins and then slows a creature; spiders are immune to webs. Fire burns the patches away.

- A damaging direct hit on a Magma Crawler spills burning ground around it for 3 turns. Fire heals the Crawler instead of hurting it, including its own flames. Periodic damage does not retrigger the spill, and fire cannot revive a dead Crawler.

- Both Stoneskin sigils purge Poison and Stun, then prevent those statuses and poison damage. Both Levitation sigils release roots and webs and let you avoid ground hazards while floating. Existing poison or burns are not cured by levitation.

- Amulet of the Tide now clears webs, flames, poison patches, slime trails, mushrooms and other ground effects from its flooded area. The cleared hazards do not return when the water recedes.

- Enemies pursue a remembered location updated by sight, hearing or a witnessed attack. Seeing you step around a corner records that step, but later unseen movement needs new evidence. Enemies follow useful diagonal routes and search nearby when they reach the last known location.

- Noticing you no longer consumes an action. An enemy that sees you at the start of its action can act immediately, and a searching move checks sight again afterward. Breaking sight around a corner or closed door can still create a surprise opening when the enemy finds you again.

- Goblin Brutes can Shoulder Check after a damaging melee hit, pushing a living target back one tile. Skeletons and Mother Murk's risen Skeletons can charge a clear gap of up to 3 tiles and strike. Both abilities have a 4-turn cooldown.

- Mother Murk's Life Drain has a 10% chance per rank on a landed attack to deal 2 shadow damage per rank, scaled by Divine Power, and heal the attacker for damage actually dealt. Offhand attacks can trigger it, and a pet's attack heals that pet. Healing respects Rot and maximum HP.

- Nature's Bounty costs one Fire, Water, Air and Earth mote plus 300 essence, is allowed for Murk, and appears in the correct sigil order at the forge.

- Wobbles' pranks become increasingly likely as Amusement falls, reaching 1 in 500 turns at 40 and 1 in 50 at 10, with a small chance even at high Amusement. Pranks add Amusement and rewards spend it; Favor does not determine these odds.

- Rebuilt the HUD for phones, tablets and PCs. Phone menus cover the status column and map; tablet and PC menus cover the map. The hotbar stays in place. Options can swap the status and log column to either side, and PC players can hide the movement pad.

- Mobile play uses landscape. If the browser cannot lock orientation, a rotate-device prompt blocks game input until the device is sideways. Phones have a compact two-column Character sheet; tablets use the desktop Equipment arrangement.

- The default Ember skin matches the title and character screens. Options adds several UI themes, three text sizes and background transparency from 0% to 90%; text, icons and status bars stay solid. Controls have softer edges, clearer frames and a quiet click sound.

- Inventory holds 25 items in five flush rows. Drag worn equipment into the bag to remove it, or drag a bag item outside the panel to drop it. Curses and a full bag still prevent removal.

- Picking up a boss core offers infusion using the same element rows and confirmation as the Forge. Gear no longer offers infusion. Ordinary crafting forges are removed from boss floors, including saved floors; normal forges and the final Forge retain their services.

- Explore is available on the HUD and the rebindable O key. It follows known safe routes and stops for enemies, damage, harmful conditions, newly discovered traps, nearby alarms and hazardous ground. Press it again to stop.

- Numpad movement bindings now stay separate from top-row hotbar numbers, with Num Lock on or off. Older numeric-only bindings can be rebound once to record their keypad identity.

- Resource hover cards, status artwork and timers are clearer. Wobbles' Amusement has its own meter; permanent passives no longer appear as active buffs. Lucky Streak, Floating, Storm Form, Poison, Rot and Web have the appropriate artwork, and Stillness, Resolve and Snuffed display their remaining duration or steps.

- Character passives no longer show redundant green on labels, and unspent stat points have a subtle pulse. Gear and character totals consistently use Divine Power. Equipping a ranged weapon adds it to an empty hotbar slot; existing assignments and later manual removal are preserved.

- Run reports can be copied from the end-of-run screen or Previous Runs. They include your build, equipment, progress and cause of death; unavailable details in older records are identified rather than invented.

- One-handed swords, daggers and ceremonial knives use a raised, angled ready stance on the map and in character previews. Slime and Sporecaller idles have intermediate frames at normal playback speed, and the Ghoul's planted claw attack uses sharper sampling without changing its timing.
